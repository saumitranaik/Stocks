  • Unified institutional recommendation engine, used everywhere in this app: Quality 35% (business quality, financial strength, profitability, growth, cash-flow quality, balance sheet) + Valuation 25% (P/E-P/B-yield-PEG vs. sector, blended with margin-of-safety from the sector-appropriate valuation model) + Technical 15% (technical scorecard) + Risk 15%, inverted (institutional composite risk score) + Relative positioning 10% (vs. sector peers), renormalized over only the buckets this data source can verify — a missing bucket is never defaulted to a neutral value
  • A stock cannot show Buy or better while its own composite risk score is elevated or its own valuation is materially overvalued, and Strong Buy requires High confidence — any such cap is shown on Company Research → Valuation's Recommendation card. Below 40% bucket-weight coverage, no composite score or rating is shown
  • Banks, NBFCs, insurers and asset managers are valued with a justified Price-to-Book / excess-return model instead of DCF (see Company Research → Valuation) — a levered-free-cash-flow model doesn't map to their balance sheets

Watchlist-derived, not a transaction ledger. Every figure below reads the same illustrative target-weight allocation as the rest of this app — there is no buy/sell/quantity/price transaction record, cost basis, or realized/unrealized P&L anywhere in this app today. Transactions (trade date, buy/sell, quantity, price, charges, cost basis, realized/unrealized P&L) is future, deferred functionality — not built, not faked here.

Allocation weights are an optional, user-entered target % per company (set via Manage → company rows). Unset companies split the remaining weight equally; the full vector is normalized to sum to 100%. An all-unset watchlist is treated as equal-weighted. These are illustrative allocation percentages, not real currency holdings.
