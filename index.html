  • Unified institutional recommendation engine, used everywhere in this app: Quality 35% (business quality, financial strength, profitability, growth, cash-flow quality, balance sheet) + Valuation 25% (P/E-P/B-yield-PEG vs. sector, blended with margin-of-safety from the sector-appropriate valuation model) + Technical 15% (technical scorecard) + Risk 15%, inverted (institutional composite risk score) + Relative positioning 10% (vs. sector peers), renormalized over only the buckets this data source can verify — a missing bucket is never defaulted to a neutral value
  • A stock cannot show Buy or better while its own composite risk score is elevated or its own valuation is materially overvalued, and Strong Buy requires High confidence — any such cap is shown on the Valuation tab's Recommendation card. Below 40% bucket-weight coverage, no composite score or rating is shown
  • Banks, NBFCs, insurers and asset managers are valued with a justified Price-to-Book / excess-return model instead of DCF (see the Valuation tab) — a levered-free-cash-flow model doesn't map to their balance sheets
  • 10-year financials, ownership history, ratios and sector classification for India from Screener.in; prices and technicals from Yahoo Finance's public chart feed; recent company news from Google News RSS
